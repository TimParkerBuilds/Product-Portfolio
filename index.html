The AI inside the platform

Claude-based skill

The renewal package gives a rep AI-generated insights for each area of an account, drawing on product usage data, customer signals, and external news. A link out to Claude lets the rep go deeper on a single customer.

My part

I contributed to the prompts, reviewed the outputs, and directed the engineers who built the skill. Reps reviewed the outputs during development.

Problem: insights that were not insightful

Early on, the AI insights were not valuable to a rep. We fixed that by changing the prompts, with reps reviewing the outputs as we went.

Problem: the skill would not always run

Data connectors would disconnect and leave the skill without its inputs. We reconfigured it to confirm the data was connected first, and to prompt the user to reconnect when it was not.

Align Technology · Business Consultant · Jul 2019 – May 2022

Watch the work before recommending anything.

Problem
Patients would arrive for an appointment and their Invisalign aligners had not been ordered or had not arrived. Practices ran on manual paperwork, and neither staff nor owners could easily tell how the business was performing.
Approach
I sold and delivered 12-month consulting engagements to solo orthodontists, multi-location groups, and large DSOs, all customers outside the company. I sat with the staff and learned how the work got done before recommending anything.
Shipped
A digital process that let the team quickly see each order's status and follow up with the manufacturer or the patient before the appointment. I also ran P&L analysis to give owners insight into their business.
Result
In one engagement, the P&L analysis helped the owner determine that their marketing spend was not delivering the expected ROI.
  • On-site discovery with solo, multi-location, and DSO customers
  • 360-degree assessment: P&L, marketing, operations, technology
  • Moving analog workflows to digital workflows
  • Setting KPIs so owners and staff can see performance

/Building with AI

Small tools, built to solve my own problems.

These are personal projects, separate from the work above. Neither is groundbreaking. Each one fixes a real problem I had, and each taught me something about working with AI.

Simple agent · In use · Built with Claude

RunLayer

Problem
As a runner in North Carolina, I often see the temperature and weather change mid-run. I would end up too hot or too cold, and I could never remember what I wore in similar conditions.
How it works
It runs on its own schedule, checks the local weather, and recommends an outfit from the apparel and accessories I own.
What I changed
I had it start capturing the date. After each run I now chat with Claude about how the apparel and gear worked, so the next recommendation has that feedback.
In progress · Built with Claude

Job fit scorer

Problem
A job search turns up more openings than anyone can apply to well. I needed a quick, consistent way to tell whether a role is worth applying for, given my background and preferences.
How it works
It reads a job description against my background and preferences and returns a score out of 10, with the strengths and gaps named. It separates gaps I can address in a cover letter from gaps I cannot close, and recommends skipping a role when two or more of the second kind show up. Pay is noted as context and does not change the score.

I have 14+ years in operations, strategy, and product across SaaS, medical devices, and life sciences. For the last several years that work has been product: defining requirements, running UAT, and shipping internal platforms, most recently at ServiceNow.

I am curious by nature, and my career shows it. I started as a US Marine, moved into genomics labs as a researcher and lab manager, then into operations, consulting, and product. Each move came from wanting to understand how something worked and make it work better.
